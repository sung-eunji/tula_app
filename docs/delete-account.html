Account Deletion Request

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Tula users can delete their account directly in the app. This page explains where to find the delete action and what data is removed.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Tula app and sign in.
  2. Go to My Page.
  3. In Help and legal, tap Request Account Deletion.
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt.

What Is Deleted

  • Account profile data (email, nickname, metadata)
  • Members, memberships, classes, attendance records, and sequences linked to the account

Retention Exceptions

Some limited information may be retained for a period required by law, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate operational obligations.
