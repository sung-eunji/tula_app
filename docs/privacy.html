Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tula collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use the Tula mobile application and related services.

Questions?

If you have any questions or privacy concerns, contact us at nebaneba.rt@gmail.com.

1. What Tula Does

Tula is a business and record management tool for yoga instructors. It helps instructors organize member trends, class records, teaching sequences, and professional notes in one place.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account information such as email address, password, nickname, and gender
  • Member information such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, and notes
  • Business records such as products, memberships, class schedules, attendance, and saved sequences
  • Messages or support requests that you send to us by email

Sensitive information

We do not intentionally process sensitive personal information.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide core app features such as members, schedules, products, and attendance
  • To save and manage yoga sequences you create manually or with AI assistance
  • To respond to support requests and service inquiries
  • To improve the app, troubleshoot issues, and protect the security of the service

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service providers to operate the app:

  • Supabase for authentication, database storage, and backend services
  • Google Gemini API for AI-assisted yoga sequence generation when you use AI features

5. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside your place of residence, including locations used by our third-party service providers. We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information.

6. How Long We Keep Information

We keep your information for as long as your account remains active, unless a longer retention period is required by law or needed for legitimate business purposes such as resolving disputes, preventing abuse, or complying with legal obligations.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Tula is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. You may also have the right to object to certain processing or withdraw consent where applicable.

To make a privacy-related request, you can email us at nebaneba.rt@gmail.com or use the following data subject request form: Termly DSAR Form.

10. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Tula account from within the app. When you delete your account, we may delete or anonymize associated profile and business records, subject to legal or operational retention requirements.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
